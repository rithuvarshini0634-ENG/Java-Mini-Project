Assessment instructions

Answer the questions based on the situations described. Each question has one best answer. Submit your responses to receive your score, correct answers, explanations, and skill-wise results.

This is one combined assessment portal. MCQs are the question format; the assessment is the complete evaluation that measures your understanding and identifies topics that need further practice.

Your results are saved using the learning progress system in this frontend. They are not automatically stored in MySQL unless your backend supports that operation.
